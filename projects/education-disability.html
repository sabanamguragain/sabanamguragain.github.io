Nepal Education & Disability Inclusion

Children with disabilities are statistically invisible in Nepal's enrolment data. This interactive dashboard sets the recorded 0.94% against expected prevalence to expose an identification failure, then traces the why, built from CBS Census 2021, MoEST Flash Reports, UNICEF and UNESCO-UIS, applying the Washington Group Child Functioning framework.

What it shows

The headline is the gap: 0.94% of primary enrolment recorded as children with disabilities, against an estimated 5–10% prevalence, an identification failure rather than an absence. The dashboard then traces the why, physical barriers, untrained teachers, and a persistent gender gap that widens at higher levels.
