Nepal Economic Indicators

A five-year read on whether Nepal's economy is strengthening. I compiled official annual statistics from Nepal Rastra Bank into a structured six-sheet workbook, then built this interactive dashboard across GDP, inflation, the external sector, government finance, monetary policy and the capital market (FY 2020/21–2024/25).

What it shows

A single scan of the macro picture. Growth and reserves are up, inflation and lending rates are down, and the capital market has recovered, while the trade deficit, though improving, stays large. Built to be read by a non-specialist in under a minute.
